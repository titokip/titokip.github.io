How to Play

Master the rules in three quick steps

  1. Drag black balls to form a line of 6 either horizontally, vertically, or diagonally to win.
  2. Move one ball at a time, you cannot jump other balls or cells.
    ✓
  3. Wait for your opponent to counter each turn. The game lasts until someone wins.
