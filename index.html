Simple rules, deep strategy. Here is everything you need to know.

Line up six

Drag your black balls to form a line of six, across, down or diagonally, to win.

One step at a time

Move one ball per turn into a neighbouring empty cell. You cannot jump over other balls.

✓

Beat the AI

Your opponent answers every move, so block its lines while you build your own. The game lasts until someone wins.

Questions

Quick answers before you play.

Is Sixline free?

Yes, Sixline is free to download and play.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. The whole game works offline.

How is it different from Gomoku or Connect 6?

Sixline is inspired by classic line-up games, but it has its own rules. You need six in a row, and instead of placing new stones you slide your existing balls one step at a time on a 6×6 board.

How do I win?

Be the first to line up six of your balls in a row, across, down or diagonally. You can't jump over other balls, so block your opponent's lines while you build yours.

Which devices does it run on?

Sixline is built for Android phones.
